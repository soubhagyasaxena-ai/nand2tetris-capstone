Part A · Hardware Description Language

Hardware logic highlights

  • Bit 15 selects an A-instruction (0) or a C-instruction (1).
  • Bit 12 selects A or M as the ALU's second input.
  • Bits 11–6 drive the ALU: zx, nx, zy, ny, f, no.
  • Bits 5–3 choose the destinations A, D and M.
  • Bits 2–0 choose the jump: (j1 AND ng) OR (j2 AND zr) OR (j3 AND positive), only when bit 15 is set.
  • Address bits 14 and 13 select RAM16K, the Screen, or the Keyboard at 24576.

    How the keyboard polling works
    
      
    The program reads RAM[24576]. If a key is down it sets the colour to -1 (black), otherwise to 0 (white). It then repaints 4 words in each of 20 rows, starting at 17632 with a stride of 32.
